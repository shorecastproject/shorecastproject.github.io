Consortium

  • BRGM i the French Geological Survey. One of his activities focusses on coastal risks of erosion and flooding (prevention, forecast, crises management, adaptation) within the context of climate change. It develops expertise in field observations, remote sensing, shoreline modeling, sea-level rise, uncertainty quantification and metamodeling.
  • ISAE-SUPAERO is a leading French institute of higher education and research in aerospace engineering. Within its DISC department, the Applied Mathematics team develops mathematical and computational methods for modeling complex systems, with a focus on numerical modeling, uncertainty quantification and metamodeling, and applications to aerospace and environmental challenges.
  • IMT (Toulouse Mathematics Institute) is a CNRS Research Laboratory which federates the mathematics community of the Toulouse area. The participants of the SHORECAST project from this Institute are member of the IMT team Statistics and Optimization. This team led for instance the ANR COSTA BRAVA project on Complex spatio-temporal dynamics analysis by model reduction and sensitivity analysis and is strongly involved in the ANITI cluster.
  • Waeles Marine Consultants (WMC) is a private engineering company specialized in hydrodynamic processes, sediment dynamics, and morphodynamics of coastal (beaches, bays, estuaries, lagoons, ports, etc.) and offshore (continental shelves and slopes) areas. Its expertise is based on the analysis of physical processes, numerical modeling and in-situ measurements. Within SHORECAST, WMC team is mainly involved in wave numerical modeling (design of computer experiments, validation, simulations) and coastal management actions (development of a GIS plug-in).
  • EPOC is a research laboratory dedicated to understanding the functioning and evolution of marine and continental environments. Within EPOC, the METHYS team investigates the evolution of sandy coasta through field observations, remote sensing, numerical modeling, from the timescales of storms to climate change impacts.
