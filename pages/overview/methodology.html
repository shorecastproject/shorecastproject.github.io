Methodology

SHORECAST consists of five interconnected Work Packages (Fig. 1) and requires strong interactions between the contributors and disciplines. WPs 2 and 3 include the core methodological developments. They will need datasets and local site knowledge (WP1) and will be the basis for the achievement of WP4 focused on bringing the developments to an operational level for uncertainty quantification. As SHORECAST aims to develop new methods to address key climate change adaptation questions for coastal managers and stakeholders, among the application sites (named “Regular site” in Fig. 2), two (Soulac, Le Barcarès) will serve as a DEMO sites for which workshops will be organised with local coastal zone managers. WP0, dedicated to project coordination, dissemination and interaction with coastal managers, will guarantee necessary and fluid communication and regular interactions between WP’s via meetings and work sessions. Besides, an external advisory committee (see) will monitor and back up the project progresses and orientations.
