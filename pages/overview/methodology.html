Methodology

SHORECAST consists of five interconnected Work Packages and requires strong interactions between the contributors and disciplines. WPs 2 and 3 include the core methodological developments. They will need datasets and local site knowledge (WP1) and will be the basis for the achievement of WP4 focused on bringing the developments to an operational level for uncertainty quantification. Several application sites have been pre-selected, located mainly in France, but also in Spain, USA and Sri Lanka. There are both local (few kilometers long) and regional (tens of kilometers long) sites. As SHORECAST aims to develop new methods to address key climate change adaptation questions for coastal managers and stakeholders, among the French application sites, at least one serve as a DEMO sites for which workshops will be organised with local coastal zone managers.
